# FE-94.5 | Move App's header block into AppHeader behind an explicit props contract

[← PLAN.md](../PLAN.md) · 🌱 Starter (filed 2026-08-23)

## 🌱 Starter context

_Captured 2026-08-23 during the `/ft-refactor viz/src/ui/App.tsx` depth survey — promote to full tasknote at `/ft-task` checkout._

### Why this exists

Lines 325-428 of `App.tsx` — roughly a fifth of the file — are one `<header>`
carrying seven unrelated concerns: the logo/title, the stats caption with its
four warning badges, the list/board toggle, the search input (holding the
`searchInputRef` that `useKeyboardNav` focuses on `/`), the shortcuts button,
`ThemeToggle`, the settings button, and `ProjectSelector`.

Moving it is the largest single reduction available and forces the header's
inputs to be named rather than ambiently closed over — which is what makes
FE-94.6's state extraction tractable. It runs after FE-94.4 so it carries
already-extracted badge components rather than seven inline spans.

### Solution shape

- `viz/src/ui/AppHeader.tsx` receives an explicit props contract covering what
  the header actually reads: `activeProject`, the four counts
  (`total`/`filteredCount`/`inProgress`/`starterCount`), `projectVersions`,
  the three diagnostics arrays plus `liveDisconnected`, `viewMode` +
  `onViewModeChange`, `query` + `onQueryChange`, `searchInputRef`,
  `onOpenShortcuts`, `onOpenSettings`, and the `ProjectSelector` inputs
  (`projects`, `latestRelease`, `onSelectProject`).
- `searchInputRef` passes through as a prop — `useKeyboardNav` stays in `App`
  and keeps the same ref object, so `/`-to-focus and Escape-to-blur behavior
  is untouched.
- The `starterSuffix` string derivation (App lines 213-214) moves with the
  header, or is passed as `starterCount` and formatted inside — decide at
  promotion, but pin it either way via FE-94.2's test.
- Consider a nested `ViewModeToggle` if the props list justifies it; not
  required.
- Acceptance seeds for Phase 1 to promote into `## ✅ Acceptance`:
  - full suite (incl. FE-94.2's additions) green before and after, unchanged;
  - behavior-preserving, no feature change, no visual change — rendered header
    DOM identical;
  - `App`'s public surface unchanged; `AppHeader`'s props are new surface
    introduced by this child, with its only call site migrated here;
  - `npm --prefix viz test`, `run typecheck`, `run lint` green.

### Files to touch (preliminary survey — drift-check at promotion)

- `viz/src/ui/AppHeader.tsx` — new.
- `viz/src/ui/App.tsx` — header block replaced by one `<AppHeader …/>`.

### Explicitly out of scope

- The `<main>` render ladder (loading / no-matches / board / list) and the two
  modals — they stay in `App`.
- The state that feeds the header — FE-94.6 extracts it; this child passes
  today's values straight through.
- Reading state from context instead of props. The existing contexts
  (`VisibilityContext`, `SearchContext`, `RowInteractionContext`) serve deep
  row trees; the header is a direct child and an explicit props contract is
  the point of this step.
- Restyling or class changes of any kind.

### Decisions locked in this conversation

| Decision | Choice | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Props, not context | Explicit props contract | Naming the header's real inputs is the deliverable; a new context would hide exactly what FE-94.6 needs to see |
| `searchInputRef` | Passed as a prop; `useKeyboardNav` stays in `App` | Keeps the keyboard-nav wiring and its ~157 pinned tests untouched |
| Sequence position | After FE-94.4 | Carries composed badges instead of duplicated spans |

### Open at promotion (Phase 1 should resolve)

- Does the props list justify splitting `ViewModeToggle` (and possibly
  `HeaderStats`) out of `AppHeader`? Lean: split `ViewModeToggle` only if the
  header exceeds ~120 lines after the move.
- `starterSuffix` formatted in `App` or in `AppHeader`? Lean: in `AppHeader` —
  it is presentation, and FE-94.2 pins it at the DOM.
