**Discovery Notes:**

**Root cause (debounce).** Trailing debounce coalesces quiet-period flushes
well but defers indefinitely while events arrive faster than `debounceMs`.

**Glob metachar (optional tail).** Chokidar interprets `*`, `?`, `[`, … in watch
path strings. Escape the literal base path, leave the trailing glob unescaped.
