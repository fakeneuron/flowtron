**Implementation Notes:**

`starterSuffix` moved into `AppHeader` (derived from a `starterCount` prop) per the Phase 1 assumption. `searchInputRef` passes through as `React.RefObject<HTMLInputElement | null>` with `useKeyboardNav` still mounted in `App` holding the same ref object, so `/`-to-focus and Escape-to-blur are structurally untouched.
