**Discovery Notes:**

**Source read.** `viz/src/ui/App.tsx` is 336 lines: 31 imports, ~20 lines of
module constants + one `diagnosticMessage` helper, ~145 lines of hook wiring and
derived memos (`:51-196`), ~138 lines of JSX (`:198-336`). It is a composition
root — no domain logic, no inline components. `viz/src/ui/useProjectData.ts` is
243 lines holding two responsibilities: scoped fetch orchestration with
per-scope monotonic seq guards (`:53-137`) and the SSE connection lifecycle with
recovery polling (`:139-213`).

**Candidate seams, derived independently before the archive skim.** Four:
(1) extract the SSE lifecycle as `useLiveConnection(refresh) -> liveDisconnected`
(75 lines, one-arg boundary); (2) collapse the three `DiagnosticBanner` call
sites plus the two single-instance status strips into a status region (~72
lines); (3) lift the derived-memo block into a `useTaskViewModel` (~35 lines);
(4) extract the main-content branch as `<BoardBody>` (~34 lines).

**Archive skim (probe over 70 `App.tsx` hits + 24 `useProjectData` hits).** All
four candidates already have explicit dispositions on record:

| Candidate | Prior disposition |
|---|---|
| `useLiveConnection` split | **FE-088.3 rejected** — "`useProjectData` already owns fetch + SSE lifecycle, so connection state belongs there rather than in a new hook" |
| Data-driven diagnostic strips | **FE-94.4 rejected** — "a behavior-shaped change, not a de-duplication" |
| Error + disconnect banners folded in | **FE-94.4 rejected** — "single-instance and shaped differently" |
| `useTaskViewModel` memo lift | **FE-94.3 locked** — "moving memo boundaries is a behavior change, not a restructuring"; re-affirmed by FE-94.6 |
