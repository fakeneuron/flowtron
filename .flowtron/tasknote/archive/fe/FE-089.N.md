**Escalation lever handed over by FE-089.2 — assessed, not fired.** `.2`'s
Testing Notes flagged that if the gate flakes again, the documented next step is
raising the *global* `asyncUtilTimeout` in `setup.ts`. Assessment at audit time:
**do not fire.** The margin is not close. `App.test.tsx` in isolation runs its 54
tests in 3.27 s total (~60 ms/test) against a 5 000 ms per-wait window and a
15 000 ms per-test cap; the full cold suite finishes in ~6 s, well under the
~12–14 s `.2` measured on a loaded machine and far under the ~26 s single-file
figure `vite.config.ts`'s comment cites from the FE-052/FE-053 era. Raising the
global now would buy no observed reliability and would blunt the timeout's
genuine-hang detection. The lever stays documented in `.2`'s archive for the
next flake, which is the right resting place for it.

**Known accepted asymmetries (not misses).**
- `vite.config.ts:150-155`'s rationale comment cites "~26 s in full-suite runs
  (vs ~340 ms per test in isolation)" for `App.test.tsx`. Today's measurement is
  ~4 s isolated for the whole file. The number is a dated observation from the
  FE-053 cut, explicitly attributed ("See FE-053"), and it justifies a value
  that is still correct and conservative — durations are machine- and
  load-dependent, so re-stamping it each time the number moves would be churn
  with no contract behind it. Left as-is deliberately.
- No `.1` Discovery tasknote — audit-repo supplied the child list (established
  local convention, [[CORE-425.N]], [[FE-088.N]]).
- The epic's PLAN line offered two levers ("widen/restructure … or amortize
  cold-start env cost"). Only the first was taken. Cold-start amortization
  (pool/isolate tuning) was correctly left unspent: at ~6 s cold there is no
