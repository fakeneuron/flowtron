# FE-94.3 | Lift App's derived selectors into a pure taskView module

[← PLAN.md](../PLAN.md) · 🌱 Starter (filed 2026-08-23)

## 🌱 Starter context

_Captured 2026-08-23 during the `/ft-refactor viz/src/ui/App.tsx` depth survey — promote to full tasknote at `/ft-task` checkout._

### Why this exists

`App.tsx` computes the whole view model inline: `matchesFilter` (lines
112-135), the child-pruning `filteredNodes` (142-150), `bySection` with its
critical-first sort (152-163), `listViewEmptySections` (165-168), `epicIds`
(170-176), `visibleIds` (178-191), and the four header counts (193-214).

All of it is pure — `Task[]`, `Map<string, Tasknote>`, `query`,
`statusFilter`, `collapsedSections`, `expandedEpicIds` in; arrays and numbers
out. None of it is unit-tested; it is only reachable through the DOM. That
makes the filtering semantics — which are subtle, per the CORE-432.3 comment
about keeping "N of M matching" and `j/k` stops consistent with the pruned
tree — expensive to assert and easy to break silently.

It sits second because pure-function unit tests bought here de-risk every
later child: FE-94.5 and FE-94.6 move the call sites, not the logic.

### Solution shape

- New `viz/src/ui/taskView.ts` exporting the selectors as plain functions,
  parameterized by their current closure inputs (no React, no hooks).
- `App.tsx` keeps the `useMemo`/`useCallback` wrappers and their dependency
  arrays exactly as they are, calling into the new module. Memoization
  boundaries do not move in this child — that is a behavior surface.
- New `viz/src/ui/taskView.test.ts` covering the semantics the DOM tests can
  only assert indirectly: parent-kept-when-child-matches with non-matching
  siblings pruned, the `filteredCount` identity (`1 + children.length` summed),
  critical-first stable sort within `High`, `visibleIds` order across
  collapsed sections and collapsed epics, `effectiveStatus` fallback in
  `inProgress`.
- Acceptance seeds for Phase 1 to promote into `## ✅ Acceptance`:
  - FE-94.2's characterization tests plus the whole existing suite green
    **before** the move and unchanged **after** it;
  - the move is behavior-preserving and carries no feature change;
  - `App`'s public surface (props-less `React.FC`) unchanged;
  - `npm --prefix viz test`, `run typecheck`, and `run lint` green.

### Files to touch (preliminary survey — drift-check at promotion)

- `viz/src/ui/taskView.ts` — new; the extracted pure selectors.
- `viz/src/ui/taskView.test.ts` — new; direct unit tests.
- `viz/src/ui/App.tsx` — call sites replace inline bodies; imports adjusted.

### Explicitly out of scope

- Changing what any selector computes, including the critical sort and the
  `displaySection` completed-overlay behavior (FE-086).
- Moving or collapsing memoization boundaries.
- The presentation extractions — FE-94.4, FE-94.5.
- The state hooks — FE-94.6.

### Decisions locked in this conversation

| Decision | Choice | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Extraction target | `src/ui/taskView.ts`, not `src/ui/utils.ts` | `utils.ts` is the small shared-helper bag; the view model is its own concern and deserves its own test file |
| Where memo lives | Stays in `App.tsx` | Moving memo boundaries is a behavior change, not a restructuring; keep this child pure |
| Layer | Under `src/ui/` | These selectors are browser-view concerns, not the shared pure-parsing tier (see [[FE-093]]) |

### Open at promotion (Phase 1 should resolve)

- Does `matchesFilter` take `(task, tasknotesById, query, statusFilter)` or a
  small options object? Lean: explicit positional args — one call site.
- Should the four counts move as one `deriveCounts()` or stay four functions?
  Lean: four, matching the four independent `useMemo`s.
