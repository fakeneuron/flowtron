# FE-94.6 | Extract App's prefs and selection state into useViewPrefs and useBoardSelection

[← PLAN.md](../PLAN.md) · 🌱 Starter (filed 2026-08-23)

## 🌱 Starter context

_Captured 2026-08-23 during the `/ft-refactor viz/src/ui/App.tsx` depth survey — promote to full tasknote at `/ft-task` checkout._

### Why this exists

The last and riskiest seam. `App.tsx` holds eleven pieces of UI state plus two
refs, and three of its effects/callbacks couple them in ways that are easy to
break by accident:

- `visibilityPrefs` re-reads per project on `activeProject` change (lines
  87-90) and writes through `updateVisibilityPrefs` (92-98); `viewMode` reads
  once from storage and writes through `updateViewMode` (100-103).
- `navigateToTask` (231-263) uncollapses the target's section, expands its
  parent epic, then inside `requestAnimationFrame` scrolls and sets a
  `HIGHLIGHT_MS` timer held in `highlightTimer.current` — with a matching
  unmount cleanup at 105-110.
- `handleSelectProject` (216-229) fans out a reset across six state setters
  plus the highlight timer plus `window.scrollTo` plus `reset()`. That fan-out
  is precisely why the state must move as a unit: split it and the reset
  silently drifts.

It runs last so it lands on a file whose selectors, badges, and header have
already moved out and whose behavior is fully pinned.

### Solution shape

- `viz/src/ui/useViewPrefs.ts` — owns `visibilityPrefs` + `viewMode`, the
  per-project re-read effect, and both persistence writes
  (`writeVisibilityPrefs`, `writeStoredViewMode`). Takes `activeProject`.
- `viz/src/ui/useBoardSelection.ts` — owns `expandedId`, `selectedId`,
  `highlightId`, `collapsedSections`, `expandedEpicIds`, the `highlightTimer`
  ref and its cleanup, `navigateToTask`, and a `resetForProjectSwitch()` that
  `handleSelectProject` calls. Takes `tasks`.
- `App` composes both, keeps `query`/`statusFilter`/`settingsOpen`/
  `shortcutsOpen`/`searchInputRef`, keeps `useKeyboardNav` and the
  `rowInteraction` memo, and keeps the render ladder + modals.
- The `rowInteraction` context value must keep the same identity semantics —
  its memo deps span both hooks, so re-render behavior is a real surface here.
  The row/subtask expand-toggle, highlight, and navigation tests are the guard.
- Acceptance seeds for Phase 1 to promote into `## ✅ Acceptance`:
  - full suite (incl. FE-94.2's additions) green before and after, unchanged —
    especially `App — navigateToTask`, `App — project switching`, the per-project
    prefs/density/palette tests, and `App — completed-bucket grouping`;
  - behavior-preserving, no feature change; timer and rAF sequencing unchanged;
  - `App`'s public surface unchanged;
  - `npm --prefix viz test`, `run typecheck`, `run lint` green.

### Files to touch (preliminary survey — drift-check at promotion)

- `viz/src/ui/useViewPrefs.ts` — new.
- `viz/src/ui/useBoardSelection.ts` — new.
- `viz/src/ui/App.tsx` — state block, effects, `navigateToTask`, and
  `handleSelectProject` replaced by two hook calls.

### Explicitly out of scope

- `useProjectData` and `useProjects` — already extracted, untouched.
- `useKeyboardNav` — stays wired in `App` with the same params object.
- Introducing a reducer, a store, or a new context for this state.
- Changing the highlight duration, the rAF sequencing, or the scroll behavior.
- Any leftover cleanup in `App.tsx` beyond what this move makes unused.

### Decisions locked in this conversation

| Decision | Choice | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Two hooks, not one | prefs vs selection | Different lifetimes and different inputs; prefs key on `activeProject`, selection keys on `tasks` |
| Reset lives with the state | `resetForProjectSwitch()` on `useBoardSelection` | `handleSelectProject`'s six-way fan-out is the exact thing that drifts when state and reset are separated |
| Position in sequence | Last | Highest risk; lands on a file already thinned and fully pinned |
| No reducer | Keep `useState`/`useToggleSet` | Matches the codebase's existing idiom; a reducer would be a rewrite, not a move |

### Open at promotion (Phase 1 should resolve)

- Does `query`/`statusFilter` belong in a third hook? Lean: no — they feed
  `useKeyboardNav` and the header directly and `App` stays their natural owner.
- Should `useBoardSelection` return the `rowInteraction` memo itself, or the
  parts for `App` to memo? Lean: return the parts — `rowInteraction` also
  needs `tasknotesById` from `useProjectData`.
- Does the `handleSelectProject` reset still need `window.scrollTo` and
  `reset()` in `App`, or do they move too? Lean: they stay in `App` — one is
  the window, the other belongs to `useProjectData`.
