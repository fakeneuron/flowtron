**Implementation Notes:**

Single-file change in `viz/src/ui/App.tsx` (+56 / −35 lines, net +21):

- New `WikilinkMarkdown` component encapsulates the `<ReactMarkdown remarkPlugins={[remarkGfm]} components={{ a: ... }}>` shape and the `wikilinkifyMarkdown(markdown)` preprocess. Single source of truth for wikilink-aware markdown rendering.
- `TaskDetail` no-tasknote branch: replaced inline `<ReactMarkdown>...</ReactMarkdown>` with `<WikilinkMarkdown markdown={task.description} navigateToTask={navigateToTask} />`. Outer `prose prose-xs ...` wrapper preserved.
- `TaskDetail` tasknote branch: passes `navigateToTask` through to all three `<DetailSection>` calls (Goal / Acceptance / Subtasks).
- `DetailSection` gained a `navigateToTask` prop; replaced its plain `<ReactMarkdown remarkPlugins={[remarkGfm]}>{markdown}</ReactMarkdown>` with `<WikilinkMarkdown markdown={markdown} navigateToTask={navigateToTask} />`.

Code-span exclusion (`wikilinkifyMarkdown` splits on `(\`[^\`]*\`)` and only processes even segments) carries over unchanged — wikilinks inside backticks still render as literal code. Same behavior as CORE-024's no-tasknote branch.

`navigateToTask` already handles missing IDs silently (`tasks.find(...) → undefined → skip section/epic expansion → scrollIntoView no-ops on missing element`). No additional handling needed for unresolved IDs.
